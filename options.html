Open any job posting, then click the extension to score it with TypeSafe's Jev model.

New here? Follow the setup guide.

Why this is cheap

Jev charges $0.042 per million input tokens and no output tokens. Full job descriptions vary in length, so the popup shows the estimated cost of each score.

When you click the extension icon, it sends your resume and the open page's title, company, text, and web address to api.typesafe.ai for scoring.
