Privacy policy

This covers the Worth Applying? Chrome extension. The short version: your data stays in your browser, except what goes to TypeSafe to score a job. We don't run a server and we don't collect anything.

What the extension stores

Everything below lives in your browser's local extension storage, on your device:

  • Your TypeSafe API key
  • Your resume text
  • Your settings, such as whether you want the visa estimate
  • Scores for jobs you've already checked, so the same job isn't scored twice
  • Simple counts, such as how many jobs you've scored and how many tokens that used

What it sends, and when

Only when you click the extension icon on a job page, it reads that page and sends the following to TypeSafe's API at api.typesafe.ai:

  • Your resume text
  • The job's title, company, and description from the page
  • The page's web address

When you test your API key in Options, it sends one short test request with that key. TypeSafe's own privacy policy covers what it does with what it receives.

What it never does

  • It doesn't send anything to us. We don't run a server.
  • It has no analytics, tracking, or ads.
  • It doesn't read pages unless you click its icon.
  • It doesn't sell or share your data.

Why it asks for these permissions

PermissionWhy
activeTab, scriptingTo read the job page you're on, only after you click the icon

api.typesafe.aiTo send the scoring request

Deleting your data

Remove the extension from chrome://extensions. That deletes everything it stored. To change your resume or key, edit them in Options and click Save.

Questions

Open an issue on GitHub. The code is open source, so you can check everything above for yourself.
