Try it now

Install it in about five minutes.

It's free on the Chrome Web Store. You bring your own TypeSafe key.

Add to Chrome → Get a TypeSafe key ↗ (opens in a new tab)

Your resume and key live in your browser. When you score a job, only your resume and that posting go to TypeSafe. Privacy policy

  1. Add it from the Chrome Web Store.
  2. Sign in to TypeSafe and create an API key. Its model, Jev, does the scoring for about 2 cents per 100 jobs.
  3. In the setup guide that opens, add your key and your resume as plain text.
  4. Open a job posting and click the icon.
